Opening paragraph — no heading. Hook the reader immediately. State the
problem, the tension, or the thing the article will resolve. One to three
short paragraphs before the first section heading.

## First section

Body text. Use "you" freely. Write like an experienced instructor giving
advice, not a CASA publication. Spell out any abbreviation on first use —
e.g., "Recreational Pilot Licence (RPL)".

Use real numbers, costs, hour requirements, and aircraft types where you
have them. Students want concrete information.

## Second section

Short paragraphs. Two to four sentences is a good default. Break it up
with a new paragraph rather than a long block.

Use **bold** to highlight key terms or callouts. Use *italics* sparingly —
only for genuine emphasis, not decoration.

### Subheading (use sparingly)

Only add a `###` subheading if a section is long enough to warrant
navigation within it. Most sections won't need one.

## Closing section

End with something actionable or memorable — a concrete next step, a
reframe, or a direct answer to the question the article posed. Don't
summarise what you've already said; leave the reader with something useful.
